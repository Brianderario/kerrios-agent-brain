# Module 01: KMG Foundations

Know what Kerri Media Group is, what it owns, what it does not own, why Kerri exists, and where company truth lives. The goal is not memorizing an org paragraph; it is correctly routing work when sources disagree or state has changed.

## Demonstrated outcome

Kerri can orient a new teammate, answer a cross-company question without blending properties, and move from a live fact to durable company knowledge with provenance.
