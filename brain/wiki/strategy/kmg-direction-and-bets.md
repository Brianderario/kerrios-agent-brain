# KMG Direction And Strategic Bets

scope: strategy synthesis · created: 2026-07-09 · owner: Brian · status: current synthesis, not a new decision

This page gives humans and agents one routed picture of where Kerri Media Group is going. It synthesizes existing decisions and vision records; it does not turn an unapproved idea into policy.

## Enduring direction

- Build a focused media and events company around trusted, high-value audiences in hardware, manufacturing, and the industrial base.
- Grow Hardware FYI and Kinetic as owned properties while preserving the legal and information boundary around Standard & Works.
- Use Savant and Kerri to increase revenue and execution capacity without requiring a large conventional operating team.
- Keep Brian as the approval point for consequential external actions while expanding agent autonomy only through earned, measured evidence.
- Make company knowledge useful to both people and agents through permissioned, source-backed records instead of private chat memory.

## Current business bets

1. **Hardware FYI revenue machine.** Inventory, sponsor products, pipeline, fulfillment, reporting, and renewals should operate as one closed loop against the live company goal.
2. **Events as a revenue and relationship engine.** Kinetic and related programs deepen community trust, create differentiated sponsor value, and generate reusable relationship intelligence.
3. **Audience and relationship data as the moat.** KMG should understand who the audience is, which accounts matter, and how sponsors and readers interact, while avoiding phantom precision.
4. **Savant as the operating system.** Tasks, approvals, CRM, revenue, inventory, agent work, and the permissioned brain converge in one human-and-agent surface.
5. **Kerri as a compounding worker.** Kerri should perceive, reason, act within gates, record durable truth, surface strategic implications, and improve the workflow after repeated misses.
6. **Optional productization later.** Savant may become a product for small B2B media operators only after it reliably prints money and reduces Brian-hours inside KMG.

## Strategic decision lens

For any meaningful proposal, Kerri should state:

- which property and goal it serves;
- the source-backed current state;
- the expected revenue, audience, relationship, or execution leverage;
- what scarce resource it consumes, especially Brian-hours;
- what must be true for the bet to work;
- leading indicators, downside, and a reversible first test;
- whether this is decided, proposed, inferred, or still unknown.

## Open strategic questions

- Which parts of the KMG operating model should become repeatable products versus remain internal advantage?
- What is the right portfolio shape beyond Hardware FYI, Kinetic, Savant, and the Standard & Works partnership?
- Which audience and sponsor data creates real defensibility without adding operational drag or privacy risk?
- Where should human taste, judgment, and relationships remain the product rather than be automated?
- What autonomy should Kerri earn next, based on verified outcomes rather than aspiration?
