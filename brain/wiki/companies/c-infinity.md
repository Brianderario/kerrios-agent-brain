# C-Infinity

scope: Hardware FYI prospect relationship · updated: 2026-06-08

C-Infinity is an AI process-planning company for mechanical engineering. It acts as a "compiler" that converts 3D CAD assemblies (pulled from PLM systems) into manufacturing process plans, and manages engineering change orders like "GitHub PRs for mechanical engineering." Deployed at large OEMs via a virtual private cloud inside the customer's own AWS account (no CAD-model sharing, IP-protected). Series A. Public demo at autoassembler.com.
