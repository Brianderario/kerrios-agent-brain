## Xometry audience bucket map (H0033 — proposal in flight, 2026-06-17) {#xometry-audience-bucket-map}

Context: Xometry (on-demand manufacturing marketplace) is repackaging a stalled **$17.5K** newsletter+2-webinars offer into **vertical-specific, experiential** activations for H2 2026. Source: 6/15 Granola call (`c6240547-…`) + Madison Ledwith's brief *"Xometry x HardwareFYI Partnership - H2 2026 - Audience Breakdown.pdf"* (brian@ thread, 6/17). They explicitly do **not** want broad newsletter blasts ("Protolabs and Fictiv already run those") — they want named verticals, named accounts, and "champions." Their objectives: (1) credibility for order growth, (2) awareness of full offering (injection molding/production, not just CNC), (3) **identify + nurture champions to grow account penetration.** Persona across all buckets: **engineers, technical founders, and buyers (procurement/ops)**, 25–40, highly educated.

Their five target buckets, mapped to named accounts **already on our list** (counts = subscribers; "eng/founder" = share of that bucket who are technical or founder-level — exactly the champions Xometry wants):

1. **Aerospace & Defense** — *engineers + buyers at A&D companies.* **~612 subs, 60% eng/founder.** Marquee: Lockheed Martin (24), Anduril (24), Northrop Grumman (17), Blue Origin (15), SpaceX (15), Boeing (14), Relativity Space (11), NASA/JPL (11), Skydio (6), Astranis (6), Raytheon/RTX (8), Collins Aerospace (4), L3Harris (4), Sierra Space (4), Stoke Space (4), Hadrian (4), Shield AI, BAE, GE Aerospace.
2. **Hardware / Robotics** — *technical engineers & founders at Series A/B startups.* **~213 robotics-named subs, 71% eng/founder.** Marquee: Anduril (24), Zipline (9), Boston Dynamics (6), Skydio (6), 1X (4), Figure (4), Apptronik (3), Reliable Robotics (3), GrayMatter (3), Matic (3), Carbon Robotics (2), Standard Bots (2), Reflex (2), Symbotic, Agility — plus the broader hardware-startup base.
3. **Medical Devices** — *engineers at scale-up startups & mid-market OEMs.* **~155 subs, 78% eng/founder** (highest technical density of any bucket). Marquee: Medtronic (8), Boston Scientific (4), Intuitive (4), Stryker (3), Insulet (3), Tandem Diabetes (2), Vicarious Surgical (2), PROCEPT BioRobotics (2), Edwards, Dexcom, BD, Baxter, Integer.
4. **Data Centers** — *enterprise DC OEM buyers, engineers, facility/infra architects.* **~85 named subs, 72% eng/founder** (smallest bucket — set expectations). Marquee: NVIDIA (16), Intel (10), Eaton (8), AMD (6), Schneider Electric (3), Broadcom (3), Marvell (2), Arista (2), Cerebras (2), Astera Labs (2), Supermicro, Equinix, Groq, Ampere, JetCool (liquid cooling).
5. **Other Start-Ups & Scalers** — *engineers, buyers, founders Seed–Series B.* **~4,037 subs at sub-250-employee companies, 54% eng/founder** — the broadest, deepest bucket and our core. Includes the manufacturing dev-tool peer set: Cofactr (13), AllSpice (12), Duro (8), Jiga (6), Markforged (8), Instrumental (3), Modelwise (3), Hadrian (4), Dirac.
