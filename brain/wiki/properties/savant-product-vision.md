# Savant Product Vision (ambitious, Brian-requested)

scope: vision · created: 2026-06-12 · owner: Brian / Kerri · status: vision draft, not committed roadmap

Brian asked (2026-06-12 interactive) for the most ambitious version of Savant: needle-moving, grounded in KMG's actual business, no grandstanding. Full text below as delivered. Treat as strategic input, not approved scope; build items get green-lit individually.

## Thesis

KMG sells the trusted attention of hardware decision-makers; all products are containers for it. Breakout media businesses (Morning Brew, Industry Dive, Workweek) industrialized three things with 30-100 people: knowing the audience precisely, pricing/filling inventory like airline seats, and proving performance so renewal is the default. Ambitious Savant is those people as software with Brian as the only approval gate. The constraint on growth is Brian-hours; Savant exists to maximize revenue per Brian-hour. $1M with 3 people is 2026; same 3 people at $3-5M is what the machine is for.

## The ten pillars

1. **Inventory + yield engine.** Every sellable unit (every Tue/Sat primary placement for 12 months, partner program seats, webinar dates, Kinetic booths/dinners) is a named object: sold, reserved, or aging. Sell-through %, realized vs rate card, revenue per send. Agents work aging slots as work items. Packages assemble from real scarce inventory (structural support for the no-price-negotiation posture). Never send with an unsold primary placement unless chosen.
2. **Audience graph (the moat).** Enrich every subscriber into person+title+company; roll up to account map. Sell audience composition not impressions ("412 readers match your ICP; 38 target accounts with 3+ weekly readers; 11 have open DFM job recs"). Account-level sponsor reporting ("engineers at Anduril clicked twice"). Permanently solves Tier B demographics as a live self-regenerating page. Readers' employers = best prospect list. Compounds with list growth; uncopyable.
3. **Signal-driven demand engine.** Triggers replace lists: funding rounds, demand-gen hires, job-posting spikes (Apollo already provides), competitor-newsletter sponsorships, reader clusters forming at one company, sponsor click-spikes (upsell signal). Every outbound opens with a true "why now"; pipeline reorders daily by composite intent.
4. **Full-cycle autonomous deals.** Every deal is a state machine agents advance; Brian only approves/denies/edits. Signal → research → draft → approve → send → follow-ups → reply classification → quote from live inventory per playbook → DocuSign → Stripe invoice on signature → deliverable scheduling → asset chase → report → armed renewal. Metric: touches per dollar (target <5 per $12.5K deal vs ~30 today). Verbal-yes is a state the machine chases until papered+billed (nTop/Loombotic = the case study).
5. **Proof-of-performance engine.** Automated live campaign reports per sponsor (mid-flight, benchmarked; Protolabs report becomes a feature, not a favor). Renewal case file accrues from day 1 (performance, warm replies, booth visits, transcript moments); at T-minus-60 the year-two email drafts itself with receipts (Jiga = manual version of this).
6. **One truthful screen.** "Will we hit $1M" with no invented numbers: booked (reconciled vs Stripe/Mercury, exceptions named), weighted pipeline (weights learned from own history), inventory forecast at current sell-through, renewal book with probabilities, and the gap decomposed with what agents are doing about it this week. Phantom-pipeline rule is the design principle.
7. **Pricing intelligence.** Every quote/win/loss by package shape, price, category, size. Learn the demand curve (category willingness-to-pay; package-composition effects). Quarterly evidence-based rate cards. Won/lost reasons auto-captured from threads. (ProfitWell discipline applied to media inventory; possible because the CRM reads the threads.)
8. **Kinetic on the same machine.** 2026 survey is the spec: enriched "who visited your booth" lead packages within 48h (6/9 sponsors asked; justifies 2027 price increase), early attendee-composition visibility per tier (5/9), prospectus tiers ($60K/$30K/$10K) as yield-managed inventory, one-click newsletter↔event cross-sell packages, live event P&L (Westin $150K F&B min as tracked liability).
9. **Agent org chart.** Named roles with KPIs: SDR (signals/first touch), AE (quotes/closes), AM (delivery/reporting/renewals), RevOps (hygiene/forecast/reconciliation), Analyst (Monday memo). Self-grading exists; the 2026-06-12 approve/deny self-improvement loop becomes the management system. Autonomy granted role-by-role as track records earn it (info@ pattern extended).
10. **Endgame option: Savant as a sellable product** for 1-5 person B2B media operators ($300K-$5M on spreadsheets). Dogfooding story = GTM (beehiiv-out-of-Morning-Brew arc). Sequencing discipline: only after it prints money for HWFYI/Kinetic; build as if another operator might use it (costs ~nothing now).

## Sequencing

- **Now-90d (closes the $421K gap):** 2026 inventory objects + sell-through; renewal case files armed for everything expiring through Q1-2027 (nTop, Loombotic, EMI, Jiga); automated sponsor reports (Protolabs first, then template); the truthful $1M screen with gap decomposition.
- **3-12mo (changes the slope):** full subscriber enrichment → audience graph (do it with the beehiiv migration); signal-stack outbound; quote-to-cash automation; Kinetic 2027 graph-powered sponsor products.
- **Years (compounding):** pricing intelligence/demand curve; learned pipeline weights; full agent org with earned autonomy; optionally Savant-for-operators.

**Manage by five numbers:** revenue per Brian-hour, touches per dollar, inventory sell-through, net revenue renewal rate, reply rate on signal-triggered outreach.

## Why it works at KMG first

Every pillar is a pattern proven at scale (ad platforms, publishers, revenue software) compressed to a 3-person company because the labor is software. The novel part nobody else has: the CRM reads the threads, attends the meetings, and holds the pen.
