# HWFYI Sponsor Reply Templates

scope: workflow · updated: 2026-05-25

Reusable reply shapes for Hardware FYI sponsor conversations. These are not canned copy. They are structures Kerri should adapt to the exact thread, buyer goal, relationship temperature, and Brian-as-sender voice.

## Product-education reply after sponsor asks "what does this actually include?"

Source pattern: H0001 Aris Machina, Brian sent 2026-05-25 23:39 ET.

Use this when:

- the sponsor already has rough pricing or a menu
- the sponsor is confused about format, deliverables, or terms
- the sponsor asks for examples/case studies
- the sponsor has a clear goal, but is not yet ready for a firm package

Do not use this when:

- it is a first-touch cold email
- the sponsor has already picked a package and needs contract/procurement details
- the better answer is a one-line operational clarification

### Shape

1. Open with the call frame, not a formal intro.
   - Say the best next step is a call because product mix depends on budget, target audience, timing, and goals.
   - Keep it conversational: "I think it's best to discuss this on a call..."
   - Include Brian's calendar near the end, not as a hard CTA before answering the questions.

2. Answer their numbered questions directly.
   - Preserve their order if they numbered them.
   - Use plain definitions and examples.
   - Include links inline.
   - Do not over-package or over-price in this section.

3. Deconfuse terminology with a human aside.
   - If terms like "send-out", "publication", "placement", or "issue" created confusion, say simply what they mean.
   - A light apology is fine when our wording caused the confusion.

4. Use a comparable sponsor carefully.
   - Name the closest analog.
   - Explain why it is analogous.
   - Do not claim private performance details unless the sponsor has approved disclosure.
   - It is okay to use renewal/return sponsorship as social proof when true.

5. Introduce the broader product set as "other products you may be interested in".
   - This is education, not a package proposal.
   - Group by goal: lead generation, content, events, newsletter presence.
   - Include proof points where approved and useful.

6. Close with a call ask and optionality.
   - "Let me know when you have some time this week..."
   - Include Brian's calendar.
   - Offer to answer more questions.
   - Sign `Brian` only. No formal signature block.

### Template

```text
Of course and I think it's best to discuss this all on a call, as I can not only provide the info of each product but provide package options around your budget, target audience, and goals. As far as your questions above, see the answers below, as well as a brief description of our full product list for <year>:

1. <Product A> is <plain-language definition>. <One sentence on what it is not, if there was confusion>. <Who supplies creative / what HWFYI supplies>. A live example is here: <link> (<where to look in the example>).

2. <Product B> is <plain-language definition>. <Cadence / number of sends / what upgrades or variants are included>. <Example link or where to look>. <Who supplies creative>.

- <Terminology aside: "send-out/publication" just means <plain term>. Sorry for the confusion and we send it out <cadence>.>

3. The closest comparable sponsor is <Company>, a <why analogous>. While I can't talk to the specifics of their performance, <approved social proof, e.g. they returned as an event sponsor / renewed / expanded>. I hope this speaks to how well the partnership is going.

Here are the other products we offer that I think you'd be interested in:

<Product / category>: <1 short paragraph with proof point or why it maps to their goal>.

<Product / category>: <1 short paragraph with audience size, lead list, attendee/registrant numbers, or timing>.

Let me know when you have some time this week to jump on a call together and I'm adding my calendar here in case that's easier: https://app.reclaim.ai/m/brian-derario/hardware-fyi-meeting

Let me know if I can answer any other questions!

Brian
```

### H0001-specific lessons

- Brian's final send removed formal greeting lines and started immediately with "Of course..." because the thread already had momentum.
- Brian kept the numbered answers from Kerri's draft, but made the surrounding frame more natural.
- Brian did not use the 3-package menu in the email. He educated on products and left package construction for the call.
- Brian reintroduced custom content because the Drafter example and 5,000+ form-fills were the strongest lead-generation proof point.
- Brian grouped SF Tech Week and happy hours together because both are in-person lead-generation/event products.
- Brian used "while I can't talk to the specifics of their performance" before the CoLab proof point. Use this move whenever case-study evidence is private.
- Brian was comfortable with some rough/imperfect live-sounding phrasing. Do not over-polish sponsor replies into consultant prose.
