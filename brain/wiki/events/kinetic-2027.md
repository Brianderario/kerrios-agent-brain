## 2026 Sponsor Survey Findings (n = 9)

Source: [Kinetic 2026 Post-Conference Survey Responses](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1NH1RxWQz08yKAkpduTR6Kohzvg2K3Zxz4GIqBvHLTq8/edit) (Google Sheets, "Form Responses 2" tab). Analysis compiled 2026-06-08.

### Scores

| Metric | Avg | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall satisfaction | 8.4 / 10 | 7–10 | Two perfect 10s. Strong for year one. |
| Goal delivery | 3.7 / 5 | 1–5 | One sponsor rated 1/5 (EE-focused, audience mismatch). |
| Attendee fit | 3.9 / 5 | 2–5 | |
| Pre-event communication | 3.8 / 5 | 2–5 | Weakest category — biggest lever for improvement. |
| Onsite experience | 4.2 / 5 | 4–5 | Strongest category. |

Follow-ups reported: 1–2 (3 sponsors), 3–5 (4), 6–10 (1), 11+ (1). Custom dinner sponsors reported the most follow-ups.

### What to protect (worked well)

1. **Attendee quality** — the differentiator. Multiple sponsors said Kinetic delivered where other conferences don't.
2. **Curated dinners** — highest satisfaction (10/10), most follow-ups (6–10, 11+). Premium upsell pillar for 2027.
3. **Booth placement quality** — directly correlated with sponsor satisfaction. Be intentional.
4. **Brian + Benji as partners** — personal touch cited multiple times.

### Improvements for 2027 (ranked by frequency × impact)

| # | Action | Mentions | Effort | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Post-event lead recap package** (attendee list w/ titles, "who visited your booth") | 6 | Low | High |
| 2 | **Earlier attendee-list visibility** (company-level, 2–3 weeks pre-event, opt-out for attendees) | 5 | Low | High |
| 3 | **More private dinner / workshop options** (expand curated dinner slots 2–3×, add workshop track) | 5 | Med | High |
| 4 | **Better pre-event sponsor briefing** (single "Sponsor Playbook" PDF, 3 weeks out: floor plan, attendee demo, logistics, programming expectations) | 4 | Low | High |
| 5 | **Separate booth area from sessions; put F&B near booths** (foot traffic died during talks; food draws organic traffic) | 3 | Med | High |
| 6 | **Structured sponsor-attendee matchmaking** (curated intro email connecting sponsors w/ 5–10 target attendees pre-event) | 3 | Med | Med |
| 7 | **Tiered booth placement choice** (let sponsors pick location by tier) | 1 | Low | Med |
| 8 | **Recruit more EE-focused attendees** (audience leaned heavily ME; one sponsor asked "how many EEs?" — one hand went up) | 1 | Med | Med |
| 9 | **Don't schedule sponsored content before lunch** (crowd thinned out significantly) | 1 | Low | Med |
| 10 | **Content / media capture add-on** (scheduled photographer/videographer time for sponsor sessions) | 2 | Low | Med |

### Audience gaps flagged by sponsors

- **Electrical engineers** — audience leaned heavily mechanical. Problem for EE-focused sponsors (PCB, electronics, embedded). Either recruit more EEs or set expectations with those sponsors.
- **OEMs and autonomy companies** — one sponsor specifically named Rivian, Waymo, Kodiak, Joby, Archer, Samsara, Tesla, Zoox as targets.
- **VP Eng / Head of TA, Series A+** in space, defense, robotics.
- **Senior mechanical engineering leaders** (managers, heads-of).

### Content flag

One respondent noted John McEleney's session felt too much like self-promotion. Set clear speaker expectations for 2027: insight-driven talks, not pitches.
