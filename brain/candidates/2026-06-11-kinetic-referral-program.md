## How a conference referral program works (mechanics)

- **Unique attribution per attendee** — give every registrant a unique referral link or code at sign-up so credit is unambiguous. Luma, Eventbrite, or a UTM + "who referred you" form field all support this.
- **Tiered incentive** — refer 1 = swag / session perk; refer 3 = discount or free ticket; refer 5+ = VIP or speaker-dinner access. For a low-cost or free event, the reward is **status and access**, not cash.
- **Two-sided** — the referred person also gets something (priority registration, a discount) so the ask is easy to make.
- **Leaderboard** — a visible top-referrers board drives the competitive push; lands well with an engineering audience.
- **Timing** — launch the moment registration opens; remind at each milestone.

## Recommended shape for Kinetic

**Attendee-gets-status, referred-gets-priority.** Our audience cares more about who is in the room and access than about a discount. Lead rewards with access/status (reserved seating, speaker meetups, VIP) rather than price cuts.
