## Evidence

- **Creator format that wins (Seb, Linas):** one story per post. Hook = the single most concrete number, in line 1. One-to-two sentence paragraphs. A bullet "number ladder" (→ or emoji). A short human reaction line ("Crazy stuff.", "Read that last line again."). Then an IDENTICAL CTA line on every post (Seb: "Interested in European Tech? Sign up to my newsletter here: …"). Cadence 2-4 posts/day. Engagement 100-500+ reactions/post.
- **Linas variant:** ends with a thesis flip ("Maybe the real AI labor shock does not start at the bottom…") instead of a question. No "what do you think? 👇" bait anywhere on the high performers.
- **Alex Lieberman variant:** operator essays + raw personal one-liners (a 1-sentence post about losing his dad got 89 reactions / 23 comments). Builds the person, not the page. The person is the moat.
- **Person > page (our own data):** Hardware FYI company-page digest posts get 2-47 reactions at 7.9K followers. Pari Singh's PERSONAL post about Kinetic got 211 reactions. The page's best content all week was a repost of a human.
- **What "AI slop" looks like (the rejected draft):** 4 stories crammed into one post, long multi-clause sentences, clever-take filler ("That last line is the real story", "paying frontier prices"), tidy section transitions, engagement-bait question close. Every sentence trying to be smart = none landing.

## Proposed motion

1. **Brian's personal LinkedIn is the primary channel.** Company pages repost; they don't originate. (Optionally Mihir/Benji also post HWFYI slices from personal accounts.)
2. **Slice, don't digest.** Each Industrialist issue → 2-3 single-story posts spread across the week. Each Hardware FYI issue → same. Never 4 stories in one post again.
3. **Standing CTA, identical every post:**
   - Industrialist: "We break down the deals, contracts, and capex rebuilding American industry every week in The Industrialist. Subscribe here: <url>"
   - Hardware FYI: "20,000+ engineers, founders, and investors read Hardware FYI every week to stay ahead of the physical world. Subscribe here: <url>"
4. **Format rules:** hook number in line 1; 1-2 sentence paragraphs; one → ladder max; reaction line ≤ 6 words; thesis close, never a question-bait close; no em dashes (standing rule).
5. **Workflow:** Kerri drafts each slice into a 📣 KerriMG task (ACTION: discuss) for Brian to edit + post. Candidate automation: add a "LinkedIn slice" step to the S&W Issue Writer routine (Mon/Wed) + a HWFYI equivalent. S/W boundary holds: public newsletter content only.
