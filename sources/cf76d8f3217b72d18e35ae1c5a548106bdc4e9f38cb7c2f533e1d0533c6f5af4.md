Never engage a sponsor/customer in a back-and-forth about price (Brian rule 2026-06-11, from the NTOP thread — Brian approved that send, but flagged it as a pattern to avoid going forward).

**Why:** The price we charge is downstream of the value we create for the sponsor. A pricing debate detaches price from value, and "we always lose" that conversation.

**How to apply:** When a sponsor pushes back on price, never defend/justify/itemize the price or preemptively discount. Redirect to ROI against their stated goals (buyer audience, leads, content assets). When they don't see the value, exactly two moves: (1) prove the value with evidence (audience data, past campaign results, live issue examples), or (2) suggest a different product that delivers the value they're looking for. Reshaping the package to fit a budget is fine — that changes the value delivered, not the price of the same package. Canonical rule lives in `brain/wiki/workflows/hwfyi-package-quote-playbook.md` ("Price objections" section). Related: [[feedback-brain-log-check-before-send]].
