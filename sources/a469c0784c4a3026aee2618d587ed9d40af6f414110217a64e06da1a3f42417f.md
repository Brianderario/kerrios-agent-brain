Hardware FYI's audience is US-based, so a contact from a vendor's Europe/APAC org has near-zero chance of caring about a sponsorship. For big/global companies, do NOT accept the first Apollo hit if it is the wrong region or a weak/junior title. Do a deeper dive: refine Apollo with `person_locations: ["United States"]` + senior marketing / demand-gen / field-marketing-Americas / product-marketing / brand / partnerships titles + the relevant business unit, and if Apollo cannot surface the right person, take over LinkedIn via Chrome to find the US marketing decision-maker by hand.

**Why:** a wrong-region or junior contact converts at ~0 and signals sloppy sales work; Brian expects his sales assistant to be ambitious and finish the job both efficiently AND effectively (Brian rule 2026-06-13, after a "SOLIDWORKS EuroWest" contact slipped into the bulk vendor load).

**How to apply:** any time a contact is auto-pulled for a multinational, verify it is US-based + senior + functionally relevant BEFORE queuing it for outreach. If not, re-search (US filter) or LinkedIn-source the right person. Applies to the cold-outreach and event-vendor queues and the [[feedback_first_touch_audience_fit]] motion.
