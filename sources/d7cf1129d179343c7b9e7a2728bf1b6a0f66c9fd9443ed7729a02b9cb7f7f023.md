Brian, 2026-07-04, about Maria's grocery run but stated as a general rule: "on these long-running tasks I don't need 15 text messages telling me she's almost done. Maybe one when she starts and then one after 15 minutes." Separately he pinged "still going?" / "is this still running?" 5+ times that week when agents went silent too long — the failure is both over- and under-communicating.

**Why:** He wants to trust the work is happening without being interrupted, and he needs to know it hasn't died.

**How to apply:** For any task expected to run >10 minutes: one message at start (what's being done + rough ETA), one at ~15 minutes if still running (one line), one at completion with the result. Nothing in between unless blocked. Applies to me, Maria, and worth encoding in the Slack agents.
