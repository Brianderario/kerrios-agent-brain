- **PR B #448 (`a0c6e50`)** — `Contracts::PartnerProgramTemplate.ensure!` (Hardware FYI Partner Program SOW as a Liquid ContractTemplate; anchors /sc/ /dc/ client, /sh/ /dh/ HWFYI; **working wording, replace with legal master**). `prepare_contract_for_signature` tool: renders contract from deal (fee = selections sum, falls back to `deal.value`), generates PDF, builds a **DRAFT** ("created") envelope, files a `needs_approval` Console card with `source_details.contract_send`+contract_id+envelope_id.
