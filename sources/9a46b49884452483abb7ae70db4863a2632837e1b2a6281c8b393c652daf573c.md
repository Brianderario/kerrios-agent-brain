The HWFYI cold queue "morning batch comes up short" problem (e.g. 2026-06-23 came up 1/10) is a **sourcing/quality** problem, not a saturated market.

**Two root causes found 2026-06-23:**
1. `kerri-event-prospecting` floods the queue with **masked/redacted contact names** (e.g. "Marina Ol***a") that are undraftable — 169 of 194 queue entries on 2026-06-23. These also bloat the queue far over its 100-entry cap (was 218 after the Automate add). A prune of the masked-name junk is pending, and the deeper fix is at the `kerri-event-prospecting` agent (it should never queue masked names).
2. The lead-research **conference lane (lane 3)** was effectively dead because the big shows' exhibitor directories are JS-rendered (MapYourShow/Informa) and returned empty shells to plain WebFetch, so they silently "degraded" and were never mined. One show (Automate) = ~1,200 audience-fit exhibitors.

**Fix shipped 2026-06-23 (in `kerrios-agent-brain`):**
- `data/lead-research/conferences.json` Automate entry now points at the working MapYourShow JSON feed (`automate26.mapyourshow.com/8_0/ajax/remote-proxy.cfm?action=search&searchtype=exhibitorgallery`) with an extraction recipe; parse `DATA.results.exhibitor.hit[].fields` (exhname_t/exhdesc_t/boothsdisplay_la).
- `agent-prompts/kerri-lead-research/SKILL.md` Source 1 now tries the JSON `dataFeed` first and **auto-derives** the MapYourShow feed for any show whose page returns a JS shell (self-heals IMTS and other A3/Informa shows on next run).
- 2026-06-23 full enrichment sweep of all 606 on-audience Automate exhibitors (19 parallel Apollo workers, batched): **323 reachable US marketing/BD contacts found** with verified emails (~53% of all 606; ~78% of the genuinely US/in-audience subset). Gap map: 151 foreign-no-US-buyer, 63 no-marketing-contact, 40 unresolvable, 23 no-verified-email, 1 already-contacted — i.e. the real reachability gap is tiny (23); most "gaps" are foreign vendors that aren't our audience. Wrote all new contacts to `data/leads-master.json` (now ~697 leads): 181 Automate leads queued at the top of `cold-outreach-queue.json` (~2 months runway), the rest held as reserve; 31 enterprise incumbents (Belden, ZEISS, Amphenol, Aptiv, FARO, MathWorks, TE Connectivity, etc.) parked in reserve for a warmer motion, not cold. Audit files: `automate-enrich-targets.json`, `automate-enrich-wave1/2-results.json`, `data/lead-research/batches/2026-06-23-automate-wave2/3.json`.
- Queue pruned 2026-06-23: removed 171 masked-name/undraftable junk entries (backup `cold-outreach-queue.bak-pre-prune-2026-06-23.json`, cut audit `cold-outreach-queue.pruned-2026-06-23.json`). Root cause still open: `kerri-event-prospecting` needs the fix so it stops queuing masked names.

Mailbox-history dedup (SKILL HARD RULE 3a) is load-bearing: it caught Doosan (cold-emailed Jan 2026 in the Kinetic push, missed by the CRM) and disambiguated our Chroma Technology (chroma.com, VT optical filters) from Chroma ATE (chroma.com.tw, contacted Mar 2026).
