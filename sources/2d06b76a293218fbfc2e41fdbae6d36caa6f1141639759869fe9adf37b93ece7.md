Cold outreach contacts are NOT pipeline items. The relationship does not exist yet, the revenue is not coming in soon, it is just a theory of who could sponsor in the future. Brian wants cold outreach tracked and followed up on, but in the background where he can manage it when he chooses to look, not in his daily view.

**Why:** Brian gets inundated with alerts. Cold outreach follow-ups are high-volume, low-signal noise in the daily approval queue and morning brief. Flagging 59 cold sends as pipeline items dilutes attention from real revenue conversations.

**How to apply:**
- Cold follow-ups must NOT create individual Google Tasks approval items
- Cold follow-ups must NOT appear in the morning brief Approval Queue section
- Cold follow-ups SHOULD be tracked in cold-outreach-state.json with their own funnel (sent, followed-up, replied, converted)
- Cold follow-ups SHOULD be batched (like COLD BATCH today) so Brian can review when he chooses
- Only when a cold prospect REPLIES does it become a real pipeline item worth Brian's daily attention
- The weekly revenue standup is the right place for a cold outreach summary line (conversion rate, follow-up counts), not the daily queue
- Design toward autonomy for cold follow-ups specifically: Brian has expressed trust in this category
