Brian, 2026-06-18, on the Standard & Works Industrialist newsletter: accuracy is table stakes; what's missing is QUALITY WRITING in the Lead. Drafts read "choppy and middle of the road," nothing that "calls to be engaged with." If S&W is going to grow, the Lead has to be writing people actually want to read. It does not need to be a novel; it needs to be a genuinely good piece that leads into the bulleted stories.

The method Brian wants (his idea, modeled on Sam Parr's copywork course, the practice of studying/copying great writing to learn it): pick the best newsletter writers of the last 5-10 years, send sub-agents to study their actual techniques, then write, rewrite, and improve against those models so the skill compounds. Done 2026-06-18 with Matt Levine, Byrne Hobart, Doomberg, Brian Potter (Construction Physics), Packy McCormick. Distilled playbook lives at `agent-prompts/kerri-skill/references/sw-lead-writing-playbook.md` (the routine-loaded canonical; the older `brain/.local/sw-newsletter-drafts/lead-writing-playbook.md` is now just a pointer to it). Core unlock: never open the Lead on the news, open on a hook (inversion / rule-first / naive question), then isomorphism + inverted so-what. EXTENDED 2026-06-18 (build-loop deep-craft pass) with PART 2, whole-issue craft: subject/preview patterns, the "so what" bullet (end on a source-supported consequence, not the bare fact), section pacing, the WATCH line, the close, and a 10-point whole-issue rubric the writer + editor now load. The sales twin is `brain/wiki/workflows/hwfyi-sales-writing-playbook.md`.

Two aggregation rules for the bulleted sections (same date): (1) err toward MORE bullets, not fewer; a too-strict drop rule made the issue thin. (2) Each category must carry ALL the most important stories of the last couple of days within that beat, comprehensive not thinly curated. Accuracy + freshness bar unchanged; widen source coverage (trades, not just wires).

**Why:** Lead quality is the lever for newsletter growth; comprehensiveness is what a serious industrial reader expects in the roundup.

**CODIFIED 2026-06-18** (Brian: "10x better... codify, solidify this process"). Canonical reference `agent-prompts/kerri-skill/references/sw-lead-writing-playbook.md`. **Anti-slop lesson (Brian's specific cut):** even a good technique becomes slop when over-constructed. He deleted "...Washington wrote the check, the company kept the company, and the public got jobs and a factory. A stake rewrites that." — a manufactured rule-of-three capped by a cute setup-payoff line. Don't restate the hook as a tidy triad-plus-punchline; cut to the substance.
