Brian (2026-06-12, confirming the Jiga year-two deal setup): he is "not opposed to a placeholder being in there for a prospect or somebody we're working on. Just don't fill in a price and have phantom pipeline in there. It doesn't leave me any clarity on where the business stands."

**Why:** Brian reads the pipeline total as a statement of where the business stands. Estimated values (from prior-year deals, target lists, or "likely close" guesses) inflate that number with dollars that don't exist, so he can't trust it.

**How to apply:** Open deal rows freely for any real prospect or active relationship — visibility is good. Leave `value` empty/TBD until a real price has been sent or received (proposal, pricing email, contract, invoice). Canonical rule with the exact evidence standards: `brain/wiki/workflows/hwfyi-cy2026-revenue-goal.md` (dollar-value section). Related: [[feedback-no-price-negotiation]].
