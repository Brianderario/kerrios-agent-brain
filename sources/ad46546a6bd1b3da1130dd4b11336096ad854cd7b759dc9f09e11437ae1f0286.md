First-contact sponsor outreach must NOT lead with a specific product. Lead with how Hardware FYI's readers match the prospect's target audience, and signal we have a wide range of products spanning BOTH brand awareness AND lead generation. The goal of the first touch is the call, not the sale.

**Why:** Opening with a product (e.g. "buy a webinar" / "Partner Program") is attacking them with a pitch before they care; it also risks crossing the brand-awareness vs lead-generation framing prematurely. Audience fit is the universally-true hook; product/goal mapping comes later. (Brian rule, 2026-06-13, on the CY2026 gap-close outreach.)

**How to apply:** First-touch email = (1) audience-is-your-buyer line (19,000 hardware engineers, 45% open, Apple/Tesla/SpaceX/Anduril/NVIDIA readership), (2) one line that we run a range of products across awareness + lead gen, (3) soft CTA for a 15-min call / SFTW meetup. Do NOT name a specific package or quote a goal-specific product until the call surfaces their objective. The product-by-goal mapping and bundles in [[feedback_no_price_negotiation]] + the package-quote playbook apply to the POST-call quote, not the opener. Keep the two stages uncrossed.
