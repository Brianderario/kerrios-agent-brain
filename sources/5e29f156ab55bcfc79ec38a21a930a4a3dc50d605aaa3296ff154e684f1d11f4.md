**How it works, shipped PRs #453–#461 (all live on kerrihq-rails prod):**
- `SuperhumanMailboxClient` (app/services/superhuman_mailbox_client.rb): reads (messages_since→list_threads, read_thread→get_thread) AND sends (send/reply → create_or_update_draft + send_draft) the S&W mailbox over Superhuman's MCP. Superhuman is the only transport for S&W (no Graph/Gmail). Wired into `mailbox_client` + `can_use_mailbox?` (rides `can_use_superhuman?`, Brian-only) and `SendApprovedDraftsJob` (client_for/resolve_mailbox). Attachments unsupported on S&W sends (raises).
- Tagging: `mailbox_property` + `write_property` in tool_executor; ReplyHandler→ApprovalRequestBuilder thread `property:` onto approval+task; `ApprovalRequest#default_property_from_task` safety-net callback; `property_slug` on create_task/create_approval/remember_brain/remember_agent_memory; system_prompt flipped from "keep S&W out of brain" to "tag S&W standard-and-works".
- brian@standardandworks.com added to `SWEEP_DEFAULT_MAILBOXES`; all mail-tool rescues catch `SuperhumanMailboxClient::Error`.
- Safety: `Organization.default` (KMG by name, oldest fallback — UUID PKs make `.first` non-deterministic); `Organization#owners`/`#admins` scoped to the org's through-join (a multi-org owner was double-counted); KMG jobs pinned to `Organization.default`.
