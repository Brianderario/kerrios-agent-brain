Default to contractions in every piece of writing: chat, email, drafts, docs, the newsletter. you're, don't, it's, we'll, I'll, that's, can't, won't.

**Why:** Brian finds prose that never uses contractions stiff and off-putting (2026-06-19). It reads like a formal robot instead of a person.

**How to apply:** Write the way people actually talk, so contract by default. Keep the full form only when the emphasis genuinely calls for it (a rare "I will not let it send that" hits harder than "I won't"). This sits alongside [[feedback-no-emdashes]] and [[feedback-no-ai-tells]] as a standing writing-style rule, and governs the [[headcount-newsletter]] voice.
