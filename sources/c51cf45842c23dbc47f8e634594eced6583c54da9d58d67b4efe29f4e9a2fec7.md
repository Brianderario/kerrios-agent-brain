Follow-up emails must contain everything promised in full. Never send a follow-up that says "I'll send more information" or "details to follow." The follow-up IS the delivery.

**Why:** Brian doesn't manage email chains. If Kerri sends a follow-up that promises a second email with the actual information, Brian has to go do it. That defeats the purpose of having an agent handle follow-ups. Triggered by H0106 Protolabs follow-up on 2026-06-17 where Kerri sent a "following up, will send details" email when all the details were already available.

**How to apply:**
1. Before drafting a follow-up, inventory everything that was promised or discussed in the call/meeting (from transcript, notes, brain log).
2. If you have all the information and attachments needed, draft a complete follow-up that delivers everything in one email.
3. If you're missing an attachment or piece of information you can't produce yourself, email Brian requesting exactly what you need ("I need X to complete the Protolabs follow-up — can you put it on the chain or send it to me?"). Do NOT send a partial follow-up in the meantime.
4. Only send the follow-up once it's fully loaded with everything promised.
