Brian's foundational philosophy for ALL advertising/sponsorship selling on behalf of KMG (Hardware FYI, Kinetic, etc.). Told to Kerri 2026-06-18.

The core: we are never selling background display advertising that a client passively tracks metrics around. We sell deliberate access to a high-value audience the client cannot reach anywhere else. Sponsorship dollars are downstream of the value a particular campaign delivers, so every package and every line of messaging must show the client HOW we deliver value to them specifically.

Audience: Hardware FYI spans students a company would want to recruit, through technical decision-makers from startups to enterprise. The engineering side, not just the business side. Trade publications are poorly written and most hardware newsletters cover the business of manufacturing, not engineering. We always lean into that differentiated value.

Map the package to the client's actual goal:
- Software company selling into hardware companies: we are read by the decision-makers who move that purchase.
- A robotics company like GrayMatter Robotics (met 2026-06-17): we put them in front of students through CTOs for a recruiting campaign.

**Why:** Price is downstream of value; we always lose a pure pricing debate (see [[feedback_no_price_negotiation]]). Leading with audience fit and client-specific value is how the dollars get justified.

**How to apply:** In any package doc or sales email, name the specific value to THAT client and the specific slice of our audience that serves their goal. Tie every component back to their outcome (lead gen into hardware buyers, recruiting reach, category credibility). Never frame our inventory as generic display/impressions. Pairs with [[feedback_first_touch_audience_fit]] (first touch leads with audience fit) and the self-contained, decision-ready, plain-language sales-writing standard.
