The kerrihq-rails Console API (`/api/v1/deals`, and list endpoints generally) **caps results at 100 per page** and returns `meta.has_more: true` when more remain. `per_page=200` is silently clamped to 100.

**Why:** On 2026-06-30 I summed only page 1 of `/api/v1/deals` and reported CY2026 booked revenue as $283K. The real figure was **$668,422** (125 deals across 2 pages, 43 closed-won). A 2.4x undercount on a headline revenue number Brian was using to plan — exactly the kind of correctness miss he can't catch by reading code.

**How to apply:** Never sum or count a Console list endpoint from a single response. Loop `page=1,2,…` until `meta.has_more === false`, dedupe by `id`, then aggregate. Applies to revenue totals, pipeline counts, deal lists, and any figure that feeds a decision.
