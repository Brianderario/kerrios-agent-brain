Any follow-up email after Brian has had a call or meeting with a client/prospect sends from `brian@hardwarefyi.com` and is signed `Brian` — never from Kerri.

**Why:** Brian stated it as a standing rule on 2026-05-29 (sending the H0015 ATOMS / Shiv Patel follow-up): *"As a rule, whenever I do a call with a client, the follow-up email always comes from me, not from Kerri."* A call is a relationship Brian personally owns; the follow-up must read as coming from the person they just spoke with, not an assistant.

**How to apply:** Apply a Brian-sender lock automatically to every post-meeting/post-call follow-up draft (EOD-review drafts, inbox-sweep replies on a thread where Brian just met the counterpart). Kerri still sends the cold/logistics/scheduling mail she owns (e.g. venue inquiries). If the send guard blocks a Brian-sender draft, leave it for Brian to send manually — never auto-convert to a Kerri send. Canonical home: KerriOS `brain/wiki/workflows/draft-learnings.md` (2026-05-29 send-identity rule).
