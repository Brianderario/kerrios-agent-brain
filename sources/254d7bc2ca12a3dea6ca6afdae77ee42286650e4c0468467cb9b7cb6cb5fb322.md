KMG's "This Week in Hard Tech" roundup (LinkedIn + X) and newsletter hard-tech sourcing follow a fixed method, captured in the `hard-tech-roundup` skill (`~/.claude/skills/hard-tech-roundup/SKILL.md`) with a playbook and a people-map watchlist alongside it.

Hard rules:
1. Strict Monday-to-Friday window. Nothing dated before Mon or after Fri of the current week; verify every date and cut anything outside, even the biggest story.
2. Lead with milestones (startups that DID things: went critical, launched, tested, shipped, won contracts, broke ground, hit first silicon), not funding rounds.
3. Every figure dated and sourced; never fabricate a number, date, or person.
4. Down-weight incumbents; lead with startups.
5. Voice: locked banner "🛠️ This Week in Hard Tech", no em dashes, no AI tells, save-prompt CTA, newsletter links in the first comment.

Method: scan social FIRST via Brian's logged-in browser (Claude-in-Chrome, read-only) since milestones break on X before media picks them up; fan out parallel sector researchers (nuclear/fusion/energy, space/defense, robotics/manufacturing, chips/quantum/materials); verify dates yourself; cross-check the [[hard-tech-people-map]] watchlist; curate 6 to 10 startups-first items; then assemble the post + X version + a dated source table + a bench of extras.

Social access: there is no free LinkedIn/X connector. The way to read social is to drive Brian's signed-in Chrome via the Claude-in-Chrome MCP (`list_connected_browsers` → `select_browser` → `navigate`/`get_page_text`). Confirmed working 2026-06-19. Read-only by default; any social action (post/like/comment/follow/DM) needs Brian's explicit per-action approval.

**Why:** Brian wants these posts to become a recognizable signature (consistency beats cleverness), grounded in real milestones, and audit-safe. He caught a promised-but-undelivered playbook, then pushed for milestone-driven, strictly in-window content (2026-06-19).
**How to apply:** When Brian asks for a hard-tech roundup or hard-tech newsletter stories, invoke the `hard-tech-roundup` skill and follow the rules above. Relates to [[feedback_deliver_finished_asset]], [[feedback_no_emdashes]], [[feedback_no_ai_tells]].
