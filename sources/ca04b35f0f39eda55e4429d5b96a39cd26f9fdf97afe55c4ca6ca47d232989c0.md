Brian is non-technical and will not read code himself. He judges a coding task by whether the system actually works and by my account of it, not by inspecting the diff, and he may have the work audited later by former CTO Don. So: every coding task must be carried to **complete fulfillment** (no half-done work, no orphaned pieces), and I must **always verify on the backend that fixing one thing did not break another** (run the full test suite + checks, re-confirm the changed behavior, reason through side effects on adjacent systems) before calling it done. Commits, comments, and explanations must be **audit-ready** for a third party reading them cold.

**Why:** when the owner cannot inspect the code, the entire correctness burden is on me; a possible future technical audit (Don) means the work has to stand on its own without me present.

**How to apply:** before declaring any coding task done — suite + checks green, behavior re-confirmed, adjacent systems scanned for regressions, clear commit messages. If it genuinely cannot be finished, say so explicitly and stop rather than leave it looking done.
