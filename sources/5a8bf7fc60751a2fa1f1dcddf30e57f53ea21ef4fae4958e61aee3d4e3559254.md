Three incidents in one week (2026-07-01/02): twice the agent took over Brian's active Chrome while he was working ("why are you taking over my chrome use, open a new tab and work independently as you do"; "I want to do other work within Chrome"), and once it wrongly claimed it had no browser access when his signed-in browser was available ("you're logged in on my browser").

**Why:** Brian works in Chrome while agents run. Hijacking his focus costs him more than the task is worth; false "can't" answers hide capability and stall work.

**How to apply:** When browser work is needed, open a NEW tab (or dedicated window) and work there without stealing focus; never drive his active tab. If his signed-in session is genuinely required, say so in one sentence and still use a new tab. Before ever saying "I don't have browser access," check Control_Chrome / claude-in-chrome connectivity first. Related: [[feedback-corrections-become-skills]].
