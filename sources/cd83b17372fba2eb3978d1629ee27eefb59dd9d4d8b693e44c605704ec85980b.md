When staging the S&W Industrialist issue in beehiiv, the body MUST include a **"The Lead"** H2 heading between the intro paragraphs and the lead-analysis paragraphs. The canonical sent-issue structure (verified against the live 6/16 issue) is: THE FLOOR (node 0) → greeting ("Happy Thursday.") → 2 intro grafs → **"The Lead" heading** → 2 lead grafs → 6 sector H2 headings + bullets → Dealbook → close.

**Why:** On the 2026-06-18 issue I built the beehiiv BODY_HTML without the `<h2>The Lead</h2>`, so the intro and the lead analysis blended into one block. Brian caught it immediately ("Where is the lead heading? Why is the formatting off?"). The local markdown draft had `## The Lead`, but the hand-built beehiiv body dropped it.

**How to apply:** In [[kerri-sw-newsletter-writer]] STEP 8, the BODY_HTML inserted after THE FLOOR node must lead with the intro grafs, then `<h2>The Lead</h2>`, then the lead grafs, then the sector `<h2>`s. All section headings (including "The Lead") render as the same large bold H2. After staging, QA the heading sequence: it should read The Lead, Defense & Space, Semiconductors & Electronics, Energy & Materials, Manufacturing & Automation, Maritime & Shipbuilding, Supply Chain & Freight, Dealbook (8 headings).
