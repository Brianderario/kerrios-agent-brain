When triaging inbox and I detect that Brian has already replied to a thread, I must act on that information — not just note it. Either remove the task entirely or change the action to skip/no-action.

**Why:** Leaving a "draft a reply" action on something Brian already answered risks double-emailing and shows I had the right information but didn't exercise judgment. Brian called this out as the gap between chatbot behavior (flag and report) and autonomous agent behavior (detect and resolve). The H0026 AllSpice thread was the specific case — I correctly identified Brian had already replied, but still presented a draft action.

**How to apply:** During any inbox triage or task-generation pass, after checking thread state: if the latest message is from Brian (or sent on his behalf), that thread is handled. Remove it from the action list or mark it skip with a one-line reason ("already replied"). Never surface a draft/reply action for a thread Brian has already closed. This applies to all triage workflows, not just inbox — any task list where I'm recommending actions on items Brian may have already addressed.
