The CRM is NOT a complete record of who Hardware FYI has talked to. Inbound contacts, event sponsors, and newsletter relationships frequently never get a CRM company row, so a CRM-only dedup misses them. Before adding a company to a prospect/cold-outreach list OR cold-emailing a contact, **search the email mailboxes** (brian@, kerri@, info@hardwarefyi.com via their `search_email`, inbox AND sentitems; plus brian@kerrihq.com Gmail `search_threads`) for the company domain and the contact's name. Also cross-check the **Kinetic sponsor roster** (`brain/candidates/2026-05-24-kinetic-2026-sponsor-roster.md` — anyone Brian sent a "THANK YOU - Kinetic" email is an existing partner). Any prior two-way contact (they emailed us, we emailed them, a meeting, a sponsorship, a newsletter relationship) = EXISTING relationship → never cold-email; warm re-engage by Brian only.

**Why:** Cold-emailing someone we already know (e.g. Cofactr's Libby Melton, who emailed info@ directly, ended up in the cold list) is embarrassing and damages the relationship; Brian caught it. The bulk vendor load also leaked Kinetic sponsors Zoo, Hestus, First Resonance, Fictiv and Brian-emailed Navier AI because they weren't in the CRM. (Brian rule 2026-06-13.)
