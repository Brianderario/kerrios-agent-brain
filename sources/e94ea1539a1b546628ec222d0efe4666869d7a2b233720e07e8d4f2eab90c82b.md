**Incident (2026-07-02, ~12:45 AM ET).** Brian approved cards from Needs Brian (`needs_approval`) and they vanished from the Savant task board. Two distinct bugs in `kerrihq-rails`:

1. **Unrecognized send verb archives approved email unsent.** `TaskNotes::SEND_ACTIONS` is an exact list; the sweep agent wrote `ACTION: send-mail`, so `sendable?` was false, the card's button read MARK DONE (not APPROVE & SEND), and approving ran `resolve_with` straight to `done`. `SendApprovedDraftsJob` also re-parses and silently skips non-sendable cards even in `agent_working`. Sean Hollister (SparkFun) outreach sat unsent ~8h. The agent self-shipped the literal fix to main (55cadbf); PR #300 adds a general fallback (`SEND_VERB_VARIANT`, any hyphenated send/mail/email verb) so the next coined variant can't recur.
2. **Send job ignored attachments.** The job sent only To/Cc/Subject/Body; cards declaring `ATTACHMENTS TO SEND: <name> (Drive ID: <id>)` went out bare. The Jiga year-two proposal told Khay two PDFs were attached with none riding. PR #300 makes the job download declared Drive files (both mailbox clients take `attachments:` since PR #294), resolve name-only declarations by exact-name Drive search, and HOLD to `needs_approval` (`held_reason: attachments_unresolved`) instead of ever bare-sending a draft that promises a file.

**Follow-ons shipped the same night:** PR #301 (Graph upload sessions; per-file limits now 25 MB Graph / 20 MB Gmail / 27 MB Drive download, so the ~11 MB media kit sends as a real attachment; verified in prod with a self-send). PR #304 (Slack pruned-session recovery, recovered from the interrupted 2026-07-01 Codex session's transcript and shipped).

**Why:** cards leaving the board on send is by design (approve parks in `agent_working`; the job sends within ~2 min and archives). "Disappeared" cards therefore need a sent-proof check before resurrecting: `resolution_payload.applied_at` + the mailbox's Sent folder. Never re-open an applied card as sendable; that risks a double-send.

**Recurrence (2026-07-11, Dassault/SOLIDWORKS card 3728e36d):** the EOD meetings-review routine (runs locally on Brian's Mac) declared the attachment as a local Mac path in the 📎 block; the Render send job treated the whole path as a Drive filename, found no match, held the card `attachments_unresolved`. Root cause: local drafting agents had NO written attachment contract, and the legacy local worker that could read Mac paths retired 2026-06-30. **How to apply:** when a task-board card "disappears" after approve, check in order: (1) `resolution_payload` for `applied_at`/`applied_note` (it sent; verify in Sent folder), (2) the ACTION verb against `TaskNotes` send intent (unsendable-parse archives it), (3) `held_reason` (numeric_ground_truth / send_unconfirmed / attachments_unresolved bounce it back to Needs Brian with an event).
