# Company context and source priority

Savant is the company brain and CRM of record. The local `brain/` wiki and sanitized GitHub seed are historical/offline sources. They are not superior to current native evidence.

| Question | Current source |
|---|---|
| Company knowledge, decisions, ownership | Savant knowledge records with provenance |
| Company, person, deal, relationship | Savant CRM; match existing records before creating anything |
| Approval or task status | The exact Savant task and its resolution |
| Sent email, signature, payment, publication | Native provider proof, reconciled to the corresponding Savant record |
| Local work in flight | Relevant `NOW.md` entry and bounded recent `brain/log.md` context |
| Historical/offline context | Relevant archived wiki page or sanitized seed; label its date and limitations |

Search by the task's company, person, or topic; fetch the matching record and its evidence. Do not load the entire repo map, wiki, or seed before each action. Use the installed semantic connector or an existing repository helper supported by the current runtime; verify its available operations rather than guessing an old endpoint.

Write compact, source-backed company facts through the registered Savant identity under the current KMG playbook. Preserve source date and citation. If a write is unavailable, keep evidence in the owning task/CRM receipt and report the specific blocker. Do not fall back to growing the archived wiki or impersonating another identity.

For CRM bookkeeping, update the existing record when native evidence proves a stage change, then verify it. Hold new commercial judgments, uncertain money/approval/sent state, and conflicting material facts for resolution. An offline company snapshot can help identify an existing record; never mint a new customer ID or register a company while its authoritative lookup is unavailable.

No raw transcripts, full mail dumps, credentials, personal health data, or Standard & Works private operations, finances, compensation, or content drafts enter KMG knowledge or automation state. Never treat a chat assertion as proof that an external action happened.
