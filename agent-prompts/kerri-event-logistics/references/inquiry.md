# Venue or vendor inquiry

Follow the parent skill and current KMG playbook. Prepare a complete Savant draft; do not send it automatically.

1. Resolve the event scope and target company/contact. Reuse its existing customer/job ID. Use only confirmed or explicitly tentative dates, attendance, format, constraints and an approved shareable budget signal.
2. Search the intended sender mailbox for prior correspondence and read the relevant complete chain. For an existing chain, preserve exact newest message ID, mailbox and conversation/thread ID. Use a verified new-message route only after a real search found no chain. Missing route proof means a route-repair task, not a new-thread workaround.
3. Choose the approved identity that owns the relationship: Kerri for operational coordination or Brian for his existing relationship. For S&W-side work use its approved identity and transport and keep its draft out of KMG state. Do not infer authorization from a historical venue example.
4. Draft a concise inquiry in the sender's voice with the actual event, specific availability/service/pricing questions, relevant requirements and one useful next step. Include a budget only when approved for disclosure. Do not promise an unapproved date, headcount or spend.
5. Create/update the existing Savant approval task with validated structured draft fields, `needs_approval`, deterministic external reference, sender/recipient identity, exact reply route and any attachments promised. Use the current tool/helper syntax. A title/body summary without a sendable payload is incomplete.
6. If the selected legacy KMG workflow uses `openInquiries`, preserve `{to, consoleTaskId, consoleExternalRef, sentAt: null}` until Savant/provider proof confirms delivery. Preserve job/card pairing required by that workflow. Never fill `sentAt` from task approval alone or send again to obtain a missing receipt.

The exact approved task is executed by Savant's deterministic sender. Reconcile its provider receipt and keep the owning event record current; do not duplicate execution or copy S&W private inquiry content into KMG brain/local state.
