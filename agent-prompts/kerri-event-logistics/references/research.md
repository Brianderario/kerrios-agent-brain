# Venue and vendor research

Use the parent skill's event scope, state and approval boundaries.

For venues, establish the date/range, city, attendance and format from current records. For vendors, also identify the category and required service. Reuse verified prior relationships from CRM and relevant mailbox history; old examples are not proof a contact still owns the account.

Research enough real options to support the decision. Confirm capacity/service fit, availability status, accessibility, amenities, neighborhood/logistics, contact details, and publicly documented starting rates or a need for a quote. Label prices and availability with source/date; do not infer a bookable rate from a generic tier.

Compare viable options against the event's constraints, explaining capacity buffer, room scale, budget, relationship and service tradeoffs where relevant. A three-option shortlist is useful when there are three good fits; do not pad the result or force ten site visits.

Save a compact comparison in the owning event's files and update an existing compatible shortlist only when meaningful. Present the strongest choices and unresolved questions. If Brian already requested inquiry preparation, continue under the inquiry procedure; otherwise finish the research without a mandatory follow-up question. No booking, contract, payment or external message is authorized by research alone.
